## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** connect my Discogs account and see, for every CD spine, candidate Discogs releases I can recognize, with manual search and a pasted release link for the ones auto-search misses, **so that** I can find the right release for every disc without Discogs rate limits ever losing work.

**This plan's slice:** once Discogs is connected, every finished scan looks up each CD spine on Discogs by itself (catalog number, then artist and title, then free text) and stores up to 10 candidates per spine. A big batch is paced, and when Discogs says "slow down" the work waits and resumes without losing anything. Connecting backfills the ~237 spines already scanned. Plan 03-04 shows these candidates on the cards.
