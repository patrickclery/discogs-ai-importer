## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** connect my Discogs account and see, for every CD spine, candidate Discogs releases I can recognize, with manual search and a pasted release link for the ones auto-search misses, **so that** I can find the right release for every disc without Discogs rate limits ever losing work.

**This plan's slice:** the owner connects their real Discogs account on their own machine (port 3001) and watches their ~237 scanned spines get matched. This settles the open questions the fake Discogs server could not answer: whether Discogs sends the owner back by itself, which sign-in exchange form it accepts, and whether real search results carry covers.
