## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** connect my Discogs account and see, for every CD spine, candidate Discogs releases I can recognize, with manual search and a pasted release link for the ones auto-search misses, **so that** I can find the right release for every disc without Discogs rate limits ever losing work.

**This plan's slice:** when Discogs says "slow down" in the middle of a big batch, the whole lookup lane waits and resumes on its own without losing or failing anything. A revoked sign-in costs one request instead of hundreds, and searches for spines that vanished cost nothing. The pacing gate is pinned at every threshold.

<objective>
Add 429 deferral on the durable queue (JobDeferredError, deferJob, the lane gate in the worker), the remaining search_spine branches (not connected, revoked, vanished spine, 5xx, 4xx), and unit tests for the queue, worker, handler and pacing gate.

Purpose: the durable half of DISC-02; D-05 (pacing gate limits) and D-06 (a 429 never loses work, retries up to 3, then Search failed).
Output: queue and worker changes, the handler branches, laneGates, search-spine/queue/worker/gate tests, the fake's 429 tuning and verify-discogs-search steps (e) and (f).
</objective>
