## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** connect my Discogs account and see, for every CD spine, candidate Discogs releases I can recognize, with manual search and a pasted release link for the ones auto-search misses, **so that** I can find the right release for every disc without Discogs rate limits ever losing work.

**This plan's slice:** a card with more than three matches can show all of them; every match's details read exactly as the UI contract says; the Spines page says plainly when Discogs isn't connected, keeps refreshing while lookups run, and counts down when Discogs has asked the app to slow down.

<objective>
Add "Show all" (the candidates GET route, the shadcn Collapsible and the lazy list), pin the details-line, format and label rules and the candidate ordering in tests, and add /spines polling while searches run, the page-level not-connected Alert and the paused countdown.

Purpose: DISC-04 (every candidate's details, all candidates reachable) and the live half of DISC-03; D-04 (page notice), D-06 (paused countdown), D-10 order, D-12 Show all.
Output: the candidates GET route with its test, Collapsible, CandidateList Show all, candidate-format tests, queries tests, spinesRefetchInterval with tests, DiscogsNotConnectedAlert, the paused line and verify-discogs-search step (c3).
</objective>
