## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** connect my Discogs account and see, for every CD spine, candidate Discogs releases I can recognize, with manual search and a pasted release link for the ones auto-search misses, **so that** I can find the right release for every disc without Discogs rate limits ever losing work.

**This plan's slice:** when automatic matching misses, the owner opens "Search Discogs…" on a card, pastes a Discogs release link or number, checks that release against the crop, and adds it to the spine's candidates, where it stays on top. Plan 03-12 adds free-text search in the same dialog and every error state.

<objective>
Build the Search Discogs dialog on its paste-a-release path: the client-safe release-link parser (pinned by tests), the text and ref modes on the search route, the fetch_release handler on the discogs lane, the dialog's polling route, and the add-manual POST that copies from stored results.

Purpose: DISC-06; D-15 and the manual half of D-16.
Output: discogs-ref.ts and its tests, search route modes, enqueueExplicitSearch kinds, fetch-release.ts, the dialog polling route, the candidates POST, DiscogsSearchDialog with its trigger, and verify-discogs-search step (h).
</objective>
