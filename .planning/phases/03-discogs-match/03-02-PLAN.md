## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** connect my Discogs account and see, for every CD spine, candidate Discogs releases I can recognize, with manual search and a pasted release link for the ones auto-search misses, **so that** I can find the right release for every disc without Discogs rate limits ever losing work.

**This plan's slice:** on Settings, the owner clicks Connect Discogs, approves on discogs.com and comes back to "Connected as {username}". From the same card they can test the connection, disconnect, and reconnect after Discogs stops accepting the app; clearing a saved key disconnects too. Plan 03-04 adds the messages after coming back from discogs.com, the callback URL and the paste-code fallback.

Output: ConnectDiscogsButton, DiscogsCard, test and disconnect routes with route tests, TestDiscogsButton, DisconnectDiscogsButton, the amended settings page, actions and Clear confirm copy, and verify-discogs steps (a) and (j0).
</objective>
