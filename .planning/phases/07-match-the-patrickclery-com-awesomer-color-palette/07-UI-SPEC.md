# Phase 7 — UI Design Contract (v2: awesomer's whole look)

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Mode: autonomous. No user questions were asked. Values come from 07-CONTEXT.md (all decisions locked, including the REVISED fonts and the NEW terminal/markdown sections), 07-UAT.md gap G-07-style-1 (the owner's words), `reference/awesomer-style-rules.css` and `reference/awesomer-home.html` (exact awesomer CSS and markup), 07-PALETTE-REFERENCE.md, and the shipped code in `src/`. Values that no upstream source covers are researcher decisions marked `[auto]`. Contrast ratios use the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula. Translucent values were flattened over the surface they sit on before measuring.

**Version history**
- v1 (approved 2026-10-25): colors only. Mocha tokens, forced dark, contrast fixes C-1 to C-4. Shipped in plans 07-01 to 07-03, plus review fixes WR-01 and IN-01 to IN-03.
- **v2 (this draft, 2026-10-25):** the owner reviewed v1 (UAT G-07-style-1) and widened the scope to awesomer's whole look. **Everything v1 shipped stays exactly as it is.** v2 adds the card treatments, corner numerals, fonts, terminal/markdown touches, and contrast fixes C-5 and C-6. Sections marked **(v2)** are new or rewritten. Sections marked **(v1, unchanged)** are the shipped contract, restated so this file stays the single source of truth.

**Phase in one line:** make the app and the demo site look like patrickclery.com/awesomer: Catppuccin Mocha, dark only (shipped), plus gradient cards with faint corner numerals, Geist Mono as the UI font, IBM Plex Sans for prose, an asciiMono block wordmark, CRT scanlines, gradient page titles and markdown-style prefixes. It adds no features, data or new wording.

**Surfaces in scope:** every screen (`/upload`, `/spines` in Compact and Detailed, `/settings`, every dialog, menu, select, tooltip and toast) and the demo export, because they share components and `globals.css`.

**Unchanged from Phases 1-6:** all wording, layout structure, spacing scale, icons, component structure, URL state and behavior. v2 adds only the visual prefixes `##` and `>` (decorative, hidden from screen readers) and a decorative wordmark.

**Out of scope (deferred in CONTEXT):** a Catppuccin Latte light mode and a theme toggle. Also out (CONTEXT): awesomer's "HOTTEST" starburst and avatars.

## Typography (v2)

### Text (checked roles): four sizes, two weights

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Font |
|------|------|--------|-------------|------|
| Caption | 12px (`text-xs`) | 400 (500 for badges and labels) | 16px (1.33) | Geist Mono. Plex for prose captions (`FieldDescription`, the orphan explanation). |
| Body | 14px (`text-sm`) | 400 (500 for emphasis) | 20px (1.43) | Geist Mono. Plex for prose (see Prose list). |
| Heading | 16px (`text-base font-medium`) | 500 | 24px (1.5) | Geist Mono |
| Display | 24px (`text-2xl font-medium tracking-tight`) | 500 | 32px (1.33) | Geist Mono with `.gradient-text` (page titles) |

Weights for text: **400** and **500** only. Do not add awesomer's `font-bold` (700). Headings keep 500. IBM Plex Sans loads weight 400 only.

### Decorative glyph art (aria-hidden, not readable text)

These are pictures made of glyphs. They carry no information, are `aria-hidden="true"` and `select-none`, and are not part of the type scale:

| Element | Size | Weight | Line Height | Font |
|---------|------|--------|-------------|------|
| ASCII wordmark | 11px | 400 (the font's only weight) | 1 (11px), `white-space: pre`, `letter-spacing: 0` | asciiMono |
| Corner numeral `sm` / `md` / `lg` | 36px (`text-4xl`) / 72px (`text-7xl`) / 160px (`text-[10rem]`) | 900 (`font-black`) | 1 (`leading-none`) | Geist Mono |

The 900 weight copies awesomer exactly. It is allowed only on `CornerNumeral`.

### Prose list (IBM Plex Sans, `font-prose`)

awesomer uses Plex only through `.description`, for sentences that describe something. The rule here: **Plex applies to explanatory sentences. It never applies to data, values, labels, counts, badges, buttons, headings, titles, filenames, inputs or Claude's read.** The exhaustive v2 list:
1. `CardDescription` (in `ui/card.tsx`): every Settings card description.
2. `DialogDescription` and `AlertDialogDescription`: every dialog's explanatory text.
3. `AlertDescription`: Alert bodies, e.g. "Photos are read by the Claude CLI on this computer…".
4. `EmptyDescription`: e.g. "Claude didn't find any CD spines in this photo…".
5. `FieldDescription`: helper text under Settings fields.
6. Three app paragraphs: `ORPHAN_EXPLANATION` in `spine-card.tsx`, and in `discogs-card.tsx` "Matches appear on the Spines page…" and "Connecting opens discogs.com…".

Everything else is Geist Mono, including the page intro lines under each `h1`. Those copy awesomer's tagline (`<p class="font-mono …"><span class="text-accent">&gt;</span> Trending GitHub repos…</p>`).

### Mono width re-check `[auto]`

Geist Mono's advance is 0.6em: 8.4px per character at 14px and 7.2px at 12px. That is about 20% wider than Geist Sans. Measured consequences and decisions:

| Place | Width math | Decision |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Compact card match title (`TopMatchRow`, `line-clamp-2`) | At `xl` (4 columns in `max-w-6xl`) a card is about 264px. Minus `p-4`, the 48px cover and the 8px gap leaves about 176px, so **about 21 characters per line and about 42 in two lines**. | Keep `line-clamp-2` and the `title` attribute (full title on hover). The full title is also in Edit spine and the Detailed layout. Visual backstop below. |
| Page intro lines (`/upload` inside `max-w-3xl`) | 768 / 8.4 is about 91 characters per line, too long for comfortable reading | Each page intro `<p>` adds `max-w-[80ch]` (80 mono characters). |
| Header at 640-1023px | The logo text "Discogs AI Importer" plus the cursor at 16px is about 220px. The nav is about 280px. The longest status line ("2 queued · 1 running · 1 failed", 12px) is about 250px. That totals about 750px, over the 720px of content at `md`. | The logo text shows from `lg` (`hidden lg:inline`, was `sm:inline`). Below `lg` the logo is the mauve Disc3 icon only. Its accessible name is unchanged (`aria-label="Discogs AI Importer"`). |
| Photo-section filename, bulk bar status, badges | Already `truncate` or `flex-wrap` | No change. A wider font only wraps or truncates sooner. |
| Inputs (artist, title, catalog number) | Values scroll inside the input, as before | No change. Mono helps catalog numbers. |

## Fonts (v2)

### `src/app/fonts.ts` (exact)

```ts
import { Geist_Mono, IBM_Plex_Sans } from 'next/font/google';
import localFont from 'next/font/local';

// awesomer's fonts (07-UI-SPEC v2): Geist Mono is the UI font, IBM Plex Sans
// is for prose only, and asciiMono draws the block wordmark.
export const geistMono = Geist_Mono({
  variable: '--font-geist-mono',
  subsets: ['latin'],
});

export const plexSans = IBM_Plex_Sans({
  variable: '--font-ibm-plex-sans',
  subsets: ['latin'],
  weight: '400',
});

// A 7 KB subset of Noto Sans Mono (SIL OFL 1.1) captured from awesomer:
// U+0020-007E, U+00A0 and U+2500-259F (box drawing and block elements).
export const asciiMono = localFont({
  src: './fonts/ascii_mono.woff2',
  variable: '--font-ascii-mono',
  display: 'block',
});
```

`display: 'block'` matches awesomer (`font-display:block`). The wordmark is decorative and the file is 7 KB on the same origin, so the short invisible period is acceptable.

### Glyph coverage check (done)

`fc-query --format='%{family}\n%{charset}\n' reference/ascii_mono.woff2` returns family **Noto Sans Mono** and charset **`20-7e a0 2500-259f`**. The wordmark below uses exactly these non-ASCII code points: U+2550 ═, U+2551 ║, U+2554 ╔, U+2557 ╗, U+255A ╚, U+255D ╝ and U+2588 █. All are inside U+2500-259F, so **the subset covers the wordmark completely.** The executor's test should assert that every character in the wordmark constant is in `[ -~ ─-▟]` or is a newline.

## Corner numerals (v2)

`src/components/corner-numeral.tsx` (exact class strings, copied from awesomer's three sizes):

```tsx
const SIZES = {
  sm: '-right-1 -bottom-2 text-4xl',      // awesomer card-tiny
  md: '-right-1 -bottom-3 text-7xl',      // awesomer card-mini
  lg: '-right-2 -bottom-6 text-[10rem]',  // awesomer card-featured
} as const;

export function CornerNumeral({ value, size }: { value: number; size: keyof typeof SIZES }) {
  return (
    <span
      aria-hidden="true"
      data-corner-numeral=""
      className={cn(
        'pointer-events-none absolute -z-10 select-none font-mono font-black leading-none text-primary/[0.07]',
        SIZES[size],
      )}
    >
      {value}
    </span>
  );
}
```

Rules:
- The parent must be `relative isolate overflow-hidden`. `Card` variants `mini` and `featured` add these, and the photo-section header adds `isolate overflow-hidden` (sticky is already positioned). `isolate` plus `-z-10` puts the numeral above the card's own background but **below all content**, with no wrapper element, the same layering awesomer gets from its `relative` content div.
- It is the first child of the card or bar. The value has no `#` (the visible `#{n}` label stays as is).
- It is decorative: `aria-hidden="true"`, `select-none` (copying a card's text never picks it up), and `pointer-events-none`.
- Overflow: `overflow-hidden` clips it. A 3-digit spine number at `md` is about 130px wide in a card of 264px or more, and at `lg` about 288px in a full-width card, so it fits. Clipping is acceptable because it is decorative.
- Opacity is fixed at 7% (awesomer). Against surface0 that is 1.15:1, faint on purpose.

### Gradient page titles

## Claude's Discretion (decisions taken, open items)

v1 decisions (still in force):
1. **shadcn `--accent` = surface1, not mauve.** The brand accent is `--primary`.
2. **`--muted` = mantle `#181825`** for wells inside cards.
3. **`--secondary` = surface1** for secondary badges.
4. **New tokens:** `--primary-hover`, `--destructive-foreground`, `--selection` (v1), and `--glow` and `--cyan` (v2). No others.
5. **No `--info` token.** Sky exists as `--chart-2` and, in v2, as `--cyan`, used only by `.gradient-text`.
6. **Focus ring stays `ring-ring/50`.**
7. **Force-dark through next-themes plus a static class.**
8. **Header keeps `/80`.** `/90` is the documented fallback.

v2 decisions `[auto]`:
9. **Featured vs mini.** Featured means "the one thing in focus": the expanded card, dialogs and the bulk bar. Everything else is mini. The Detailed layout uses mini, so a long list does not glow uniformly.
10. **`card-mini` gets the featured gradient** (awesomer's mini is flat), because of the owner's quote and because it preserves every v1 contrast measurement.
11. **Numerals:** spine cards show the spine number. Settings cards show 1-4 as setup steps. Photo-section headers show the section's position. The Photos card, the bulk bar and dialogs have none. **Open:** the owner may prefer no numeral on photo-section headers (their number is positional, not an ID). It is cheap to remove at UAT.
12. **Wordmark text and placement:** two lines, "DISCOGS" over "AI IMPORTER", on `/upload` only, from 640px. **Open:** the owner may prefer a shorter single-line mark (for example "DISCOGS" alone) or showing it on every page. The header (56px) cannot hold it.
13. **Cursor blinks 5 times, not forever**, for WCAG 2.2.2. **Open:** if the owner wants awesomer's endless blink, that is an accessibility trade-off they would need to accept explicitly.
14. **No card `:focus-within` outline, no `:active` scale, no pulse, no `.card-tiny`.** Reasons are in the CSS notes.
15. **Radii unchanged** (`rounded-xl` cards, `rounded-lg` dialogs). The captured CSS sets no radius for `.card-featured` or `.card-mini`. **Open:** a squarer, more terminal-like radius is a one-token change (`--radius`) if the owner asks.
16. **Logo text from `lg`** (was `sm`) because mono is wider. Below 1024px the header shows the mauve icon only.
17. **Plex loads at weight 400 only.** Prose is never emphasized at 500.
18. **`.glow` on the wordmark** (awesomer's wordmark has none, but CONTEXT keeps the glow utilities and this is their natural home). `glow-intense` is on the logo, as awesomer does.
