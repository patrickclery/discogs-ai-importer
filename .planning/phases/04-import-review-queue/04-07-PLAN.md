## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** confirm the right Discogs release for each spine and have it land in my collection exactly once, with a status-driven list of every spine, **so that** I can work through the whole shelf to completion.

**This plan's slice:** a re-scan or a re-attach never makes an imported (or importing, or marked) spine disappear and come back as a fresh, un-imported card that the owner would confirm and add a second time. Those spines stay listed as "No longer detected" with their import, cannot be dismissed while an import exists, and are not searched again automatically.

<objective>
Extend the Phase 2 merge, re-attach and dismiss rules and the Phase 3 auto-search selectors so spines with a non-failed import or the not-on-Discogs mark are kept and left alone.

Purpose: IMP-04 (a duplicate through a re-scan is impossible) and REV-05 (a marked spine survives re-scans); RESEARCH Pitfall 5 and the carried decision that orphaned spines with a match or import are never dropped.
Output: changes to src/server/scan/merge.ts, orphans.ts, the dismiss route, DismissSpineButton and src/server/discogs/enqueue.ts, with tests.
</objective>
