## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** confirm the right Discogs release for each spine and have it land in my collection exactly once, with a status-driven list of every spine, **so that** I can work through the whole shelf to completion.

**This plan's slice:** the owner clicks "Add to Uncategorized" on the match they recognize and watches the card turn into the confirmed match: waiting its turn, being added, then in the collection. Before anything reaches Discogs they can change the match; if an import fails they see why and retry it, and a failed search retries the same way, without re-uploading the photo.

<objective>
Put the confirm control on every candidate row, replace the candidate list with the confirmed-match panel while a match is confirmed, and add Change match (cancel before start) and Retry (import first, else search) with their routes.

Purpose: IMP-01 on the page (explicit confirm), REV-04 per spine; D-01, D-02, D-13 (per spine), the D-09 failure copy.
Output: src/lib/import-view.ts, AddToCollectionButton, ConfirmedMatch, CandidateRow/CandidateList/DiscogsMatches changes, cancelQueuedJob, cancelImport/retryImport/retrySpine, DELETE /api/spines/{id}/import, POST /api/spines/{id}/retry, tests.
</objective>
