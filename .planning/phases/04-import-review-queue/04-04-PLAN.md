## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** confirm the right Discogs release for each spine and have it land in my collection exactly once, with a status-driven list of every spine, **so that** I can work through the whole shelf to completion.

**This plan's slice:** the server half of "warn me before a second copy". If the same CD appears on two overlapping shelf photos, a confirm for the second one is refused with both spines' details until the owner says "add another copy". If the release is already in their Discogs collection, the held import can be linked to that copy without any write, or turned into one more copy if they own two. Plan 04-13 puts the dialog and the decision panel on the card.
