## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** confirm the right Discogs release for each spine and have it land in my collection exactly once, with a status-driven list of every spine, **so that** I can work through the whole shelf to completion.

**This plan's slice:** "exactly once" survives the bad days. If the app is killed in the middle of an add, Discogs answers 502 after adding, or the answer has no copy number, the next attempt finds the copy the app made and records it instead of adding a second one. A release the owner already has is held for their decision, every failure ends with a reason the card can show (Plan 04-03), and an import the worker gave up on never stays Importing.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| App -> api.discogs.com add POST | Non-idempotent write that may land even when the answer is lost |
| api.discogs.com collection JSON -> adopt decision | Third-party dates and ids decide whether a copy is the app's own |
| Worker lease and process lifetime | A kill or lease loss can interrupt any step |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-09 | Tampering | ambiguous POST then worker retry | high | mitigate | Intent and snapshot written before the POST, fenced on the claim; every attempt GETs and adopts first; steps d and e prove one copy |
| T-04-10 | Tampering | adopting a copy the owner added by hand | medium | mitigate | Adopt only copies absent from the snapshot, unrecorded and dated at or after intent minus 120 s; unparseable dates never adopted; anything else is held for the owner (accept the residual: a hand-added copy of the same release within those seconds) |
| T-04-11 | Denial of service | import stuck in importing after a crash on the final attempt | medium | mitigate | markInterruptedImports maintenance hook plus the read model's interrupted fallback |
| T-04-12 | Information disclosure | error text stored on rows and jobs | medium | mitigate | Only Discogs* error messages (status, method, redacted path) are stored, sliced to 160; never response bodies; step e asserts no username in last_error |
| T-04-13 | Elevation of privilege | lease loss mid-POST | medium | mitigate | ctx.isCurrent() right before the POST and before every terminal write; intent fenced on jobs.status running and attempts |
| T-04-SC | Tampering | npm installs | high | mitigate | No package is installed by this plan |
</threat_model>
