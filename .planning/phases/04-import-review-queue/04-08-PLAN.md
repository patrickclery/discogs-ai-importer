## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** confirm the right Discogs release for each spine and have it land in my collection exactly once, with a status-driven list of every spine, **so that** I can work through the whole shelf to completion.

**This plan's slice:** for an obscure or self-released CD that Discogs does not list, the owner marks the spine "not on Discogs" from the card's menu (or straight from a "no matches" card). It leaves the to-do counts, stays in the list with its crop, and can be unmarked at any time.
