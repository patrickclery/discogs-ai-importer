## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** confirm the right Discogs release for each spine and have it land in my collection exactly once, with a status-driven list of every spine, **so that** I can work through the whole shelf to completion.

(Restated from the ROADMAP Phase 4 Goal line, which is not in user-story form; flagged in "Planner assumptions".)

**This plan's slice:** the import path end to end through the API. A confirm sent for one candidate becomes one add_to_collection job, the job reads the owner's copies of that release, writes its intent, sends exactly one add to the (fake) Discogs collection and records the copy number; `/api/spines` and the card's badge show the result. Plan 04-02 hardens the job against crashes, timeouts and 5xx; Plan 04-03 puts the Add button and the confirmed-match panel on the card; Plan 04-06 adds the folder choice.
