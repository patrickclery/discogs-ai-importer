## Phase Goal

**This plan's slice:** about 230 spines are already scanned and searched. The owner sees how much of the shelf is left at a glance (counts per status, "{i} imported · {l} left", "{i} / {n} imported" per photo) and can narrow the list to Pending, Matched, Imported, Not found, Not on Discogs or Failed, keeping the photo filter and Needs review.
