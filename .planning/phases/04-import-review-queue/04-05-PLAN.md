## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** confirm the right Discogs release for each spine and have it land in my collection exactly once, with a status-driven list of every spine, **so that** I can work through the whole shelf to completion.

**This plan's slice:** about 230 spines are already scanned and searched. The owner sees a count for every status and can narrow the list to Pending, Matched, Imported, Not found, Not on Discogs or Failed, keeping the photo filter and Needs review; when their own action moves a spine out of the filter, a toast says where it went. Plan 04-14 adds the per-status empty states and the "{i} imported · {l} left" progress on the page and on every photo header.

<objective>
Derive one review status per spine (D-10) on the server, add per-status counts, the ?status= filter, the per-photo progress data and the moved-out-of-filter explanation, and replace the All / Needs review group with the status filter plus a standalone Needs review toggle.

Purpose: REV-03; D-10, D-11 (and the D-12 default view All; Plan 04-14 renders the D-12 progress).
Output: src/lib/review-status.ts, read-model fields (reviewStatus, counts.byStatus, statusById, photoProgress), the ?status= route and page param, ReviewStatusFilter, the reworked filter bar, the moved-out toast and scripts/verify-review-queue.ts.
</objective>
