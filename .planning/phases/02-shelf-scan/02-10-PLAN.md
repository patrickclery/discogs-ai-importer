## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** drop shelf photos and see every detected spine as a horizontal crop next to the AI-extracted fields, which I can correct, **so that** misreads are easy to see and fix and each photo costs exactly one Claude call.

(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal line and the PROJECT core value; flagged in Plan 02-01.)

**This plan's slice:** the owner explicitly re-scans a photo (after a failure, a "retake closer" warning, or a model change); it is read once more, and every spine found again keeps its identity and the owner's corrections. Orphaned spines are resolved in Plan 02-11; the "Latest scan reads" hint arrives in Plan 02-12.

<objective>
Add the explicit re-scan action and the versioned merge that keeps spine identity and user edits across runs.

Purpose: INGEST-04 and D-13..D-17: re-scan is the only way a photo returns to Claude, and it must never cost the owner their corrections.
Output: the re-scan route and transaction, match/merge/levenshtein modules with fixture-tuned tests, the shared RescanButton on both pages, and scripts/verify-rescan.ts.
</objective>
