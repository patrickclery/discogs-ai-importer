## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** drop shelf photos and see every detected spine as a horizontal crop next to the AI-extracted fields, which I can correct, **so that** misreads are easy to see and fix and each photo costs exactly one Claude call.

(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal line and the PROJECT core value; flagged in Plan 02-01.)

**This plan's slice (phase tracer):** after the owner freezes the vision contract, a queued photo from Plan 02-03 is sent to Claude exactly once, the raw answer is persisted before parsing, every CD spine is cropped horizontal, and `/spines` shows each crop next to its fields. Plan 02-05 hardens failures and recovery; Plans 02-06 to 02-12 build the pages and re-scan on this path.
