## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** drop shelf photos and see every detected spine as a horizontal crop next to the AI-extracted fields, which I can correct, **so that** misreads are easy to see and fix and each photo costs exactly one Claude call.

(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal line and the PROJECT core value; flagged in Plan 02-01.)

**This plan's slice:** the owner drags shelf photos onto `/upload`, each uploads on its own, duplicates are recognized with no extra call, and anything that cannot be uploaded stays visible with a reason and a retry. It sits on the Plan 02-06 Photos list and the Plan 02-03 upload route.

Output: Dropzone, UploadView, upload-queue helpers with tests, UploadTip, row variants for client rows, loading.tsx, and the ready-state assertion in verify-claude-cli.
</objective>
