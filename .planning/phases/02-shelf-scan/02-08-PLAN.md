## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** drop shelf photos and see every detected spine as a horizontal crop next to the AI-extracted fields, which I can correct, **so that** misreads are easy to see and fix and each photo costs exactly one Claude call.

(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal line and the PROJECT core value; flagged in Plan 02-01.)

**This plan's slice:** on `/spines` the owner sees each photo as a sticky section with its status and warnings, and each spine as a card with the crop on top and Claude's read below; the list fills in by itself as scans finish. Plan 02-09 makes the fields editable; Plan 02-12 adds the Needs review and photo filters.

<objective>
Turn the server-rendered spine list from Plan 02-04 into the UI-SPEC review surface: a polled client list grouped under sticky photo headers, spine cards with the crop on top, and every section state.

Purpose: REV-01: the crop is always beside what the AI read, on every row.
Output: GET /api/spines, getSpines, review helpers, the spines page, view, section, card and crop components, and a verify-scan step.
</objective>
