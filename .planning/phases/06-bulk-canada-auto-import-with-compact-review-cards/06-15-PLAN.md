Why spacing changes too: the vendored ToggleGroup draws a joined row only at spacing 0. In that mode the items drop their left border and only the first and last items are rounded, so a joined row that wraps shows broken borders on its second line. With spacing above 0 every item keeps its own border and rounded corners, which is what lets the row wrap cleanly. Do not edit src/components/ui/toggle-group.tsx (vendored shadcn source).
