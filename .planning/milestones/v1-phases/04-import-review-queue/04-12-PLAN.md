## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** confirm the right Discogs release for each spine and have it land in my collection exactly once, with a status-driven list of every spine, **so that** I can work through the whole shelf to completion.

**This plan's slice:** the server half of "change my mind" and "try again". A confirmed match can be undone only while nothing can have reached Discogs, and a failed import or search goes back in line without re-uploading the photo, never as a second job or a second add. Plan 04-03 puts the Change match and Retry import buttons on the card that call these routes.

Purpose: REV-04 per spine (D-13), D-02 (change before start), D-07 (an unresolved intent is resolved only through Retry), IMP-01 guard (one enqueue site).
Output: cancelQueuedJob, cancelImport/retryImport/retrySpine, DELETE /api/spines/{id}/import, POST /api/spines/{id}/retry, store.test.ts and both route tests.
