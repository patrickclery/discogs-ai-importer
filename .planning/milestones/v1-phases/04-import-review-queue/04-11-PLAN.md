## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** confirm the right Discogs release for each spine and have it land in my collection exactly once, with a status-driven list of every spine, **so that** I can work through the whole shelf to completion.

**This plan's slice:** the one write the app may make to the owner's Discogs account is fenced by tests that never touch Discogs: anything other than the add-to-folder POST is refused before a request leaves the app, and no error message ever names the owner. The rules that decide what a card shows (waiting, importing, failed, whether Change match and Retry are offered) are pinned by a table, so later plans cannot drift from them.

Output: src/server/discogs/client.test.ts gate and canary cases, src/server/imports/queries.test.ts rule and mapping table.
