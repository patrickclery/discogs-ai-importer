## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** confirm the right Discogs release for each spine and have it land in my collection exactly once, with a status-driven list of every spine, **so that** I can work through the whole shelf to completion.

**This plan's slice:** the owner sees the warning before a second copy is made. If the same CD appears on two overlapping shelf photos, clicking Add on the second one shows both crops and asks first. If the release is already in their Discogs collection, the card says so (folder and date) and lets them link this spine to that copy, or add another copy if they own two. Plan 04-04 enforces both on the server.
