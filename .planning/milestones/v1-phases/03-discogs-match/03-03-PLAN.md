## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** connect my Discogs account and see, for every CD spine, candidate Discogs releases I can recognize, with manual search and a pasted release link for the ones auto-search misses, **so that** I can find the right release for every disc without Discogs rate limits ever losing work.

**This plan's slice:** once Discogs is connected, every finished scan looks up each CD spine on Discogs by itself (catalog number, then artist and title, then free text) and stores up to 10 candidates per spine, paced through the one gate. Plan 03-05 makes a 429 pause and resume without losing work, Plan 03-07 backfills the ~237 spines already scanned, and Plan 03-08 shows the candidates on the cards.
