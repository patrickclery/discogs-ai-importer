## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** connect my Discogs account and see, for every CD spine, candidate Discogs releases I can recognize, with manual search and a pasted release link for the ones auto-search misses, **so that** I can find the right release for every disc without Discogs rate limits ever losing work.

**This plan's slice:** candidate rows show real cover pictures, kept in the data folder after the first look so they work offline and travel with the folder. The header shows how many Discogs lookups are left and when Discogs has asked the app to slow down, so a big batch's progress is visible.

<objective>
Serve candidate covers from a local cache (download once from i.discogs.com, re-encode, store under DATA_DIR, serve through /api/files) and add the Discogs part of the header status (backlog, paused countdown, failed-search count).

Purpose: DISC-04 cover thumbnails (D-13) and D-07 visible backlog for DISC-02.
Output: thumbs.ts with its route and tests, the files-route and SUBDIRS allowlist entries, discogsStatus() on /api/status, pure header helpers and the updated StatusIndicator.
</objective>
