## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** connect my Discogs account and see, for every CD spine, candidate Discogs releases I can recognize, with manual search and a pasted release link for the ones auto-search misses, **so that** I can find the right release for every disc without Discogs rate limits ever losing work.

**This plan's slice:** on /spines, each card says where its Discogs lookup stands and lists the releases found, right under the crop and fields, so the owner can compare them with the real spine. Plan 03-09 adds "Show all", the page-level not-connected notice and refreshing while lookups run; Plan 03-10 adds Search again and Retry; Plan 03-11 adds the Search Discogs… dialog.

<objective>
Expose search state and candidates in the /spines read model and render them in the existing card slots: the search-state badge in the status slot and the Discogs matches section (top 3) in the footer, with every state line.

Purpose: DISC-04 (what each candidate shows) and the visible half of DISC-03; D-04 (card notice), D-10 order, D-11 states, D-12 list.
Output: SpineDto and SpinesResponse fields, src/server/discogs/queries.ts, SearchStateBadge, DiscogsMatches, CandidateList, CandidateRow, a first candidate-format helper, the slot wiring and verify-discogs-search step (c2).
</objective>
