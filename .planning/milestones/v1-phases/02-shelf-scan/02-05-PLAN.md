## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** drop shelf photos and see every detected spine as a horizontal crop next to the AI-extracted fields, which I can correct, **so that** misreads are easy to see and fix and each photo costs exactly one Claude call.

(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal line and the PROJECT core value; flagged in Plan 02-01.)

**This plan's slice:** when anything goes wrong (Claude refuses, the answer is cut off or malformed, the CLI is missing, the photo will not decode, or the app is killed mid-call) the photo shows Error with a reason the owner can act on, the raw answer is kept, and the photo is never sent to Claude a second time without the owner asking. Dense shelves get the D-03 "retake closer" flags. Plans 02-06 and 02-08 render these reasons and flags on the upload and spines pages.

<objective>
Make every scan failure visible with its reason, make crash recovery unable to spend a second call, and compute the D-03 density flags.

Purpose: D-04 and VIS-03 forbid silent drops; Pitfall 3 shows that Phase 1's retry-by-default queue would otherwise re-send photos and spend the owner's subscription quota.
Output: failure modes in the fake CLI, handler hardening, reconcile with a zero-call reparse path, density flags, upload-validation tests and a kill -9 recovery proof.
</objective>
