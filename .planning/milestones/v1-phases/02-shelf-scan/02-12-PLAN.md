## Phase Goal

**As the** owner cataloguing my CDs, **I want to** drop shelf photos and see every detected spine as a horizontal crop next to the AI-extracted fields, which I can correct, **so that** misreads are easy to see and fix and each photo costs exactly one Claude call.

(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal line and the PROJECT core value; flagged in Plan 02-01.)

**This plan's slice:** the owner narrows `/spines` to one photo or to just the spines that need a look (low-confidence or orphaned), and after a re-scan sees what Claude now reads beside any field they had corrected, with one click to take it.

<objective>
Add the D-07 filter bar with the Needs review filter, and the D-15 "Latest scan reads" hint on edited fields.

Purpose: D-07/D-09 make a long list workable by surfacing only what needs attention; D-15 shows the new AI read beside a kept user value after a re-scan.
Output: review-aware queries and helpers with tests, the filter bar, empty filter state, and the hint in SpineField.
</objective>
